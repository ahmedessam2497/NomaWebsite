Our story

Born from the heart of Egypt.

NōMA Stays is the guest platform of HAAM Management — an Egyptian hospitality company built on one belief: Um el Dunya deserves better stays.

The beginning

Um el Dunya deserves better stays.

Egypt — Um el Dunya, Mother of the World — is rising again as a top global destination. The Grand Egyptian Museum, a booming visitor economy, and a new generation of travellers who want more than sightseeing: personal, authentic stays that feel like home.

But Cairo's mid-market accommodation hasn't kept up. Between aging hotels and inconsistent short-lets there was a gap: professionally managed stays with the space of a home and the reliability of a hotel.

HAAM was founded to close that gap — sourcing properties, overseeing refurbishment and furnishing, then operating them with our own on-ground teams. NōMA and BEIT are the result.

How we run this business

Two principles. No exceptions.

(01)

The guest is at the centre of everything we do.

Every decision — how we design a room, set a rate, schedule a cleaning, answer a message — starts with one question: does this make the stay better? If it doesn't, we don't do it.

(02)

Our people are our most important asset.

Take care of your people, and they will take care of our guests. We hire carefully, train continuously and build careers — because the warmth you feel at check-in starts long before you arrive.
