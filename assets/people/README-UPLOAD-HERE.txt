OUR PEOPLE — PHOTO UPLOADS
==========================

Drop your team photos in THIS folder, then push (git add/commit/push).
Each file replaces the maroon placeholder block in the "Our people" section
on the About page. Until a file is added, that card shows a plain placeholder
with its caption — nothing breaks.

Use these exact file names (portrait orientation, JPG):

  housekeeping.jpg        ->  "Housekeeping in training" card
  front-desk.jpg          ->  "Front desk & guest care" card
  service-standards.jpg   ->  "Service standards workshop" card

Recommended size: at least 600 x 800 px (3:4 ratio). Photos are shown in
black & white automatically to match the site style.

Want more people cards or different captions? Tell Claude and they'll be added.
