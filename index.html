Good to know

Policies

For NōMA New Cairo. Mohandessin policies are on its page.

Check-in and check-out
Check-in from 2:00 pm. Check-out by 12:00 noon. Late check-out is sometimes possible; ask reception.
Cancellation
Free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival. Later cancellations and no-shows are charged the first night.
Payment
Full payment for the stay is taken at check-in. We accept credit and debit cards. Cash is possible with the approval of the management.
Children and extra beds
Extra beds and cots are available on request.
Smoking
The apartments are non-smoking. You are welcome to smoke on a private balcony or terrace. If your apartment has no balcony, please smoke outside the building.
Pets
Pets are not allowed.
ID
Every guest must show valid government ID at check-in: a national ID card for Egyptian guests, or a passport for foreign guests.
