NōMA

In Arabic, noma means sleep. Smart serviced apartments where the comfort of home meets the wonder of travel — everything you need, nothing you don't.

The model

Smart stays, honest prices.

NōMA operates whole buildings of 25+ rooms as serviced apartments — spacious studios and one-bedrooms with kitchenettes, quality essentials done perfectly, and none of the inflated extras that inflate the bill.

No breakfast buffet you didn't ask for. No gym you won't use. Instead: a real kitchen, real living space, real housekeeping, real security — at a price that makes staying weeks as sensible as staying nights.

Affordable. Spacious. Comfortable. Great locations.
