Beit بيت

Beit means home. Intimate boutique stays built around thoughtful design, warm hosting and personal care — where the city feels like home.

The idea

A home is not defined by architecture alone.

BEIT celebrates the everyday acts of living: sharing breakfast, reading by the pool, gathering around a table, playing tawla, finding an open book left behind. Hospitality through atmosphere and care — not formality and transaction.

Each BEIT is small — under a dozen rooms — more personal, more luxurious, and unmistakably Egyptian with a contemporary twist. Hosts who know your name, and days you'll plan around what's happening at the property.

Inviting. Attentive. Lived-in.
